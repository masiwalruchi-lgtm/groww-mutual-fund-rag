## Source Policy

Only official public information from the selected AMC and regulatory sources is used.

The assistant provides factual information only and does not provide investment advice.

The corpus is intended to answer questions about scheme facts such as expense ratio, exit load, minimum SIP, ELSS lock-in, riskometer, benchmark and related mutual-fund information.
