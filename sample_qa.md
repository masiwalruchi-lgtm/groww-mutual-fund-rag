# Sample Q&A – Groww Mutual Fund RAG Chatbot

These sample questions demonstrate the factual question-answering capability of the RAG assistant.

## 1. What is SIP?
SIP stands for Systematic Investment Plan. It allows investors to invest a fixed amount in a mutual fund at regular intervals, usually every month.

## 2. What is NAV?
NAV stands for Net Asset Value. It represents the per-unit value of a mutual fund.

## 3. What is an expense ratio?
The expense ratio represents the annual operating expenses charged by a mutual fund scheme as a percentage of its assets.

## 4. What is an exit load?
Exit load is a fee that may be charged when an investor redeems mutual fund units before a specified period.

## 5. What is ELSS?
ELSS stands for Equity Linked Savings Scheme. It is an equity-oriented mutual fund category with a lock-in period of three years.

## 6. What is a direct mutual fund?
A direct mutual fund plan is purchased without a distributor commission and generally has a lower expense ratio than the corresponding regular plan.

## 7. Are mutual fund returns guaranteed?
No. Mutual fund returns are not guaranteed because mutual funds are market-linked investments.

## 8. What is KYC?
KYC stands for Know Your Customer. It is an identity verification process required for financial services and investments.

## 9. What is a fund manager?
A fund manager is a professional responsible for managing a mutual fund portfolio according to the scheme's investment objective.

## 10. Can I lose money in mutual funds?
Yes. Mutual funds are subject to market risks, so the value of an investment can increase or decrease.
