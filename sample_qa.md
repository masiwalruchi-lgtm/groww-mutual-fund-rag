# Sample Q&A – Groww Mutual Fund RAG Chatbot

## 1. What is SIP?
SIP stands for Systematic Investment Plan. It allows investors to invest a fixed amount in a mutual fund at regular intervals.

Source: https://www.growwmf.in/

## 2. What is NAV?
NAV stands for Net Asset Value. It represents the per-unit value of a mutual fund scheme.

Source: https://www.amfiindia.com/investor/knowledge-center-info?zoneName=NetAssetValueNAV

## 3. What is an expense ratio?
The expense ratio represents expenses charged for managing and operating a mutual fund scheme.

Source: https://www.growwmf.in/downloads/expense-ratio

## 4. What is an exit load?
Exit load is a charge that may apply when mutual fund units are redeemed within a specified period. The applicable exit load depends on the scheme.

Source: https://www.growwmf.in/downloads/sid

## 5. What is ELSS?
ELSS stands for Equity Linked Savings Scheme. Groww ELSS Tax Saver Fund has a statutory lock-in period of three years.

Source: https://www.growwmf.in/downloads/kim

## 6. What is a Riskometer?
A Riskometer indicates the level of risk associated with a mutual fund scheme.

Source: https://www.growwmf.in/downloads/riskometer

## 7. Are mutual fund returns guaranteed?
No. Mutual fund schemes are market-linked investments and are not guaranteed or assured-return products.

Source: https://www.amfiindia.com/investor/knowledge-center-info?zoneName=riskInMutualFunds

## 8. What is the minimum SIP amount for Groww Large Cap Fund?
The minimum SIP amount for Groww Large Cap Fund Direct Growth is ₹500.

Source: https://www.growwmf.in/mutual-funds/groww-large-cap-fund-direct-growth

Groww Large Cap Fund is an active mutual fund scheme offered by Groww Mutual Fund. Current scheme information is available through Groww Mutual Fund's official KIM.
