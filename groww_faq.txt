What is Groww Mutual Fund?
Groww Mutual Fund is an asset management company offering mutual fund schemes in categories such as equity, hybrid, debt, index funds and ETFs.

What is SIP?
SIP stands for Systematic Investment Plan. It is a way of investing a fixed amount in a mutual fund at regular intervals.

What is NAV?
NAV stands for Net Asset Value. It represents the per-unit value of a mutual fund scheme.

What is an expense ratio?
The expense ratio represents expenses charged for managing and operating a mutual fund scheme. Current scheme expense-ratio information should be checked on the official Groww Mutual Fund Expense Ratio page.

What is an exit load?
Exit load is a charge that may apply when mutual fund units are redeemed within a specified period. The applicable exit load depends on the scheme and should be verified from its current official scheme document.

What is a Riskometer?
A Riskometer indicates the level of risk associated with a mutual fund scheme. Current Groww Mutual Fund Riskometer disclosures are available on the official Riskometer page.

What is Groww Large Cap Fund?
Groww Large Cap Fund is an open-ended equity scheme predominantly investing in large-cap stocks.

What does Groww Large Cap Fund mainly invest in?
Groww Large Cap Fund is a large-cap equity scheme. Its scheme characteristics include investing at least 80% of total assets in large-cap equity stocks.

What is Groww ELSS Tax Saver Fund?
Groww ELSS Tax Saver Fund is an open-ended Equity Linked Savings Scheme.

What is the lock-in period of Groww ELSS Tax Saver Fund?
Groww ELSS Tax Saver Fund has a statutory lock-in period of three years.

What is Groww Multicap Fund?
Groww Multicap Fund is one of the active equity schemes offered by Groww Mutual Fund. Current scheme details should be verified from Groww Mutual Fund's official KIM, SID or factsheet.

What is Groww Aggressive Hybrid Fund?
Groww Aggressive Hybrid Fund is an open-ended hybrid scheme that invests predominantly in equity and equity-related instruments.

What is the asset allocation characteristic of Groww Aggressive Hybrid Fund?
The scheme characteristics include an equity allocation of 65% to 80% of total assets and a debt allocation of 20% to 35% of total assets.
